Action & Energy
飛

Akira

CYBERPUNK · NEON · ACTION

Cyberpunk manga in the tradition of neon-drenched near-future cities. Detailed linework, vivid electric colors, dynamic action lines, and cinematic lighting that pulls you into rain-slick streets and glowing skylines.

飛

Shonen

CLASSIC · HEROIC · ENERGETIC

Classic shonen manga energy: vibrant bold colors, dynamic action lines, expressive faces, clean bold linework. Perfect for heroic quests, martial-arts epics, and coming-of-age adventures with big feelings and bigger stakes.

飛

Mecha

HARD-SURFACE · INDUSTRIAL · SCI-FI

Mecha manga with hard-surface mechanical detail, polished chrome surfaces, and signal-red accents. Intricate linework and dramatic scale suit stories of pilots, giant machines, and industrial sci-fi cathedrals.

飛

Golden Age

PULP · VINTAGE · FOUR-COLOR

Golden Age American comic style with bold primary colors, halftone dot shading, and heavy black outlines. The vintage four-color print look of classic pulp adventures — capes, cowboys, and daring rescues.

Dark & Moody
飛

Grimoire

DARK FANTASY · OCCULT · GOTHIC

Dark fantasy manga with heavy inking and dramatic crosshatching. Rich jewel-tone shadows and gritty atmosphere suit occult mysteries, gothic tragedies, and stories with a slow-burning dread underneath.

飛

Horror

PSYCHOLOGICAL · UNSETTLING · MENACING

Psychological horror manga with desaturated palettes broken by sharp red accents. Intricate detail and an unsettling atmosphere for tales of dread, obsession, and quiet menace that never quite leaves the page.

飛

Noir

CLASSIC NOIR · CINEMATIC · MOODY

Noir manga in stark black and white with golden amber accents. Dramatic shadows and cinematic angles cast every scene like a rain-slick city at 2 AM — ideal for detective stories, moral gray zones, and quiet desperation.

飛

Neo-Noir

MODERN · CINEMATIC · TEAL & ORANGE

Neo-noir with teal-and-orange color grading, deep cinematic shadows, and moody night atmosphere. Sleek modern rendering for contemporary crime dramas and thrillers with an edge.

飛

Ink Wash

MONOCHROME · GESTURAL · LITERARY

Monochrome ink wash with expressive brush strokes, gray gradients, and high-contrast spotting. Raw gestural energy suits literary fiction, memoir, and stark graphic novels where mood does the talking.

Soft & Sweet
飛

Romance

SHOJO · PASTEL · EMOTIVE

Shojo manga in soft pastels with pink and lavender accents. Delicate linework and expressive emotions bring romance, courtship, and quiet longing to the fore.

飛

Watercolor

TRANSLUCENT · GENTLE · AIRY

Watercolor illustration with soft translucent washes, bleeding pigment edges, and gentle pastel palette. Visible paper texture creates a light, airy atmosphere for gentle stories and dreamy passages.

飛

Storybook

GOUACHE · WARM · WHIMSICAL

Storybook gouache illustration with soft matte color and rounded gentle shapes. A warm inviting palette and hand-painted texture bring picture-book charm to any tale — fables, fairy stories, adventures for the young at heart.

飛

Chibi

SUPER-DEFORMED · PLAYFUL · COMEDIC

Chibi manga with super-deformed proportions, oversized heads and eyes, and bright candy colors. Thick rounded linework and playful comedic energy turn any book into a light adventure — classics reimagined with a wink.

Epic & Fantasy
飛

Epic

HIGH FANTASY · SAGA · GRAND

Epic fantasy manga with rich warm colors and dramatic lighting. Detailed rendering suits sagas of heroes, gods, and kingdoms carved from myth — the register where everything matters and the stakes are always cosmic.

飛

Xianxia

CULTIVATION · MYTHIC · PAINTERLY

Chinese xianxia manhua with jade greens, celestial blues, and ornate detail. Painterly digital rendering evokes mystical cultivation sagas, immortal romances, and misty mountaintops where the world runs on qi.

飛

Donghua

CINEMATIC · GOLDEN · DEPTH

Cinematic donghua with warm golden light, layered atmospheric depth, and semi-realistic faces. Rich color grading gives every scene the feel of a Chinese animated film still.

Clean & Modern
飛

Sci-Fi

RETRO SF · COOL PALETTE · CINEMATIC

Retro science fiction with a cool blue and purple palette, clean linework, and cinematic lighting. Ideal for classic space opera, techno-thrillers, and adventures set among the stars.

飛

Webtoon

MANHWA · BRIGHT · GLOSSY

Full-color manhwa webtoon with soft digital cel shading and clean vector-like linework. A bright saturated palette and glossy modern rendering make every panel feel like a scroll ready to swipe.

飛

Ligne Claire

EUROPEAN BD · CLEAN · READABLE

Ligne claire in the European bande dessinée tradition. Clean uniform linework, flat bright color fills, no hatching, and even lighting produce endlessly clear, readable compositions — adventure, exploration, quiet joy.

飛

Seinen

MATURE · NATURALISTIC · GROUNDED

Seinen manga with a muted naturalistic palette, fine detailed linework, and realistic proportions. Grounded and mature — subtle cinematic shading for character-driven, literary stories.

飛

Modern Vertigo

INDIE · PAINTERLY · LITERARY

Modern indie graphic-novel style with a muted earthy palette, painterly textures, and naturalistic faces. Moody atmospheric lighting and a literary tone suit adult drama and slow-burn tension.

Artistic & Painterly
飛

Ukiyo-e

WOODBLOCK · FLAT COLOR · EDO

Ukiyo-e woodblock print with flat layered color, bold outline contours, and muted natural pigments. Edo-period tradition brings a timeless dignity to any historical adaptation.

飛

Art Nouveau

ORNATE · ELEGANT · JEWEL TONES

Art Nouveau in the Mucha tradition: ornate flowing line, muted gold and jewel tones, and elegant flat figures. Stained-glass elegance for stories with grandeur, craft, and quiet ceremony.

飛

Oil Painting

OLD MASTER · CHIAROSCURO · IMPASTO

Classical oil painting with rich impasto texture, chiaroscuro lighting, and a deep warm palette. Old-master brushwork and dramatic atmosphere elevate epic and historical tales.
